# STORY ARCS — The Seven

> Each arc: public myth, private truth, the lie, the dead-end, and the breaking clue.
> Arcs map to the four dominances (Faith/Magic/Nature/Essence) for terrain/combat reuse.

## Shared Structure
- **Myth** — what legend says (distorted by 20–50 years).
- **Truth** — what actually happened.
- **The Lie** — the falsehood the player can disprove.
- **Dead-End** — a plausible wrong trail (Mean Streets honesty).
- **Breaking Clue** — the markable evidence that cracks it.

---

## Priest — "The Martyr" (Faith / Essence)
- **Myth:** died saving the faithful at the Breach.
- **Truth:** alive-in-legend only; his miracle is *why the dead won't rest*.
- **Lie:** that his work was pure salvation.
- **Dead-End:** a relic-trail suggesting he was murdered (he wasn't).
- **Breaking clue:** the liturgy-of-binding (ties souls to the world). *(T-4/T-5)*

## Scientist — "The Casualty" (Magic / Essence)
- **Myth:** vaporized in the blast.
- **Truth:** alive, hidden, holding the only honest records.
- **Lie:** his death itself (a staged record).
- **Dead-End:** a "grave" that contains someone else.
- **Breaking clue:** measurement logs dated *after* his supposed death. *(T-6)*

## Mage — "The Architect of Ruin" (Magic)
- **Myth:** the sole villain who opened the Breach.
- **Truth:** the one who tried to *stop* it; universal scapegoat.
- **Lie:** none of his own — others' lies bury him.
- **Dead-End:** forged "confession" that reads airtight.
- **Breaking clue:** his counter-ritual notes (an attempt to *close* it). *(T-7)*

## Paladin — "The Last Crusader" (Faith)
- **Myth:** holy warrior who contained the Breach by force.
- **Truth:** enforced a cover-up to protect the Church's legend of the Priest.
- **Lie:** that the containment was a victory, not a massacre.
- **Dead-End:** battlefield honors that hide a purge.
- **Breaking clue:** a kill-order against witnesses. *(post-Breach)*

## Necromancer — "The Plague-Bringer" (Essence)
- **Myth:** raised the dead and spread the restless-soul curse.
- **Truth:** inherited the Priest's half-miracle; manages the un-dying, doesn't cause it.
- **Lie:** withholds that the Priest is the source (to avoid being disbelieved).
- **Dead-End:** a mass grave that looks like his crime.
- **Breaking clue:** reanimation timestamps *predate* his arrival. *(T-5)*

## Tamer — "The Beastlord" (Nature)
- **Myth:** loosed monsters that ravaged the survivors.
- **Truth:** shepherds the Nature-dominant wilds; the "monsters" are Breach-victims.
- **Lie:** disbelieved rather than dishonest.
- **Dead-End:** a mauling that wasn't the beasts.
- **Breaking clue:** the creatures' Breach-wounds (planar, not feral). *(post-Breach)*

## Conqueror — "The Restorer" (power over all four)
- **Myth:** the strongman who brought order to the ruin.
- **Truth:** exploited the vacuum; sustains the myths that keep him legitimate.
- **Lie:** that he arrived to rescue, not to seize.
- **Dead-End:** gratitude-propaganda that reads as genuine history.
- **Breaking clue:** land-claims dated *before* the Breach "ended." *(T-8)*

## Critique / Scope Discipline
- **Seven arcs is too many for the POC.** Ship **three** (see `MVP_VERTICAL_SLICE.md`): Priest, Scientist, Mage — the experiment's core triangle.
- Paladin/Necromancer/Tamer/Conqueror are **full-game content**; outline only.
- Every arc has **one** breaking clue and **one** dead-end. Resist adding more — openness comes from *order of discovery*, not clue volume.
