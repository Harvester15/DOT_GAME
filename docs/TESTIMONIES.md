# TESTIMONIES — Witness Matrix

> The player reconciles these. Contradictions are the puzzle, not a bug.
> Mean Streets: no testimony is "true" alone; truth = cross-checked marked clues.

## How to Read
Each witness: **Perspective · Bias · Lie · Missing Info · Contradicts.**
"Markable" clues are kept only if the player chooses (no auto-log).

---

### The Priest (via relics/liturgy — he is a legend, rarely "present")
- **Perspective:** "I held death back out of love."
- **Bias:** sanctified by the Church; cannot admit the cost.
- **Lie:** that the miracle was pure salvation.
- **Missing info:** that it bound souls to the world.
- **Contradicts:** Necromancer (who is really the source of the un-dying).

### The Scientist (hidden; speaks through records/proxies)
- **Perspective:** "It was a measurement, not a summoning."
- **Bias:** self-preservation; fears reigniting it.
- **Lie:** his own death.
- **Missing info:** that he kept the only honest logs.
- **Contradicts:** the official "blast death" record; the Mage's blame.

### The Mage (scapegoat; martyred or vanished)
- **Perspective:** "I tried to close it."
- **Bias:** none of his own — he's buried by others.
- **Lie:** a forged confession in his name.
- **Missing info:** his counter-ritual notes exist.
- **Contradicts:** Conqueror and Church, who need him guilty.

### The Paladin
- **Perspective:** "I contained the horror."
- **Bias:** protects the Church's legend.
- **Lie:** the massacre framed as victory.
- **Missing info:** the witness kill-order.
- **Contradicts:** survivors; the Tamer.

### The Necromancer
- **Perspective:** "I manage what the miracle left behind."
- **Bias:** expects disbelief; withholds the Priest's role.
- **Lie:** by omission (hides the true source).
- **Missing info:** reanimation predates him.
- **Contradicts:** the Priest's myth.

### The Tamer
- **Perspective:** "The beasts are victims, not monsters."
- **Bias:** grief; disbelieved.
- **Lie:** none.
- **Missing info:** planar wounds on the creatures.
- **Contradicts:** Conqueror's "monster" propaganda.

### The Conqueror
- **Perspective:** "I brought order."
- **Bias:** rewrites a seizure as rescue.
- **Lie:** pre-dated land claims.
- **Missing info:** he sustains every other myth deliberately.
- **Contradicts:** Paladin (ledger), Tamer, Mage.

## Contradiction Web
Priest     <-> Necromancer   (source of the un-dying) 
Scientist  <-> "Death record" (alive vs. dead) 
Mage       <-> Church/Conqueror (guilty vs. innocent) 
Paladin    <-> Tamer/Survivors (victory vs. massacre) 
Conqueror  <-> everyone       (sustains the myths)


## Convergence Points (two marked clues = a locked fact)
- Liturgy-of-binding + Reanimation timestamps ⇒ **Priest is the source, not the Necromancer.**
- Post-death logs + staged grave ⇒ **Scientist lives.**
- Counter-ritual notes + forged confession ⇒ **Mage tried to stop it.**

## Critique / Scope Discipline
- POC uses **only the first three convergences** (Priest/Scientist/Mage).
- Keep "missing info" genuinely missing — don't backfill it in dialogue; that's the Mean Streets feel.
