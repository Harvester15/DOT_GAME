# PLOTLINE — Planelords: The Breach

> Investigation-first. Nothing here is shown to the player directly.
> Mean Streets rule: the player reconstructs this from clues, relics, notes,
> and contradictory testimony. Nothing auto-logs; dead ends are allowed.

## The Catastrophe (set 20–50 years before play)
Three ambitious minds ran one grand experiment to **defeat death and master reality**:
- **The Priest** — sought to preserve all life, to end death itself.
- **The Scientist (Gnome)** — sought to *understand* reality, to measure the soul.
- **The Mage** — sought total arcane knowledge, magic without limit.

Their experiment bound four forces into a single ritual-engine:
**Faith · Magic · Nature · Essence (souls/life/death).**

It worked too well. The engine didn't *channel* the forces — it **opened a door**
to a Chaos dimension that answered back. That door is **The Breach**.

## Why 20–50 years matters (design anchor)
| Long enough | Recent enough |
|---|---|
| Myths formed; "facts" distorted | The world is still broken |
| Many witnesses dead | Factions still suffer |
| Evidence incomplete / decayed | Survivors still live |
| Legends replaced truth | Politics still turn on it |

This gap is the *engine of the mystery*: no single living person knows all of it,
so truth only emerges by cross-checking fragments.

## The Reactive World (post-Breach rules)
Chaos made reality **responsive to dominance**. Whichever force dominates a region warps it:
- **Faith dominance** → miracles, zealotry, unnatural preservation of life.
- **Magic dominance** → unstable reality, arcane storms, bent physics.
- **Nature dominance** → runaway growth, feral wilds, the land reclaiming cities.
- **Essence dominance** → thin veil between life/death, necromancy, restless souls.

These are not factions — they are *physics*. Factions merely exploit them.

## Central Mystery
**Who or what opened The Breach — and was it an accident, a sacrifice, or a choice?**
The player's real question is not "whodunit" but **"what actually happened, and what does the truth cost?"**

## Major Twists (earned, not told)
1. **The three didn't fail — they *succeeded*.** The engine did exactly what they asked; Chaos simply had its own answer.
2. **The Priest's miracle is why the dead won't rest** — the "necromancy plague" is his mercy, not the Necromancer's crime.
3. **The Scientist is alive**, hiding the only honest records, because the truth would reignite the catastrophe.
4. **The Mage was the one who tried to STOP it** — the universal scapegoat is the closest thing to innocent.
5. **There is no clean culprit.** Every "villain" acted from love, curiosity, or faith.

## Critique / Scope Discipline
- **Cut:** a literal Chaos antagonist with a name/face. Keep Chaos as unknowable *physics*; a named demon turns mystery into a boss hunt.
- **Cut:** detailed cosmology of the Chaos dimension. The player only needs *effects*, not lore tourism.
- **Keep:** the four-dominance rule — it does double duty as **worldbuilding AND combat/terrain modifier**.
