# ENDINGS — Interpretations, Not Verdicts

> No clean good/evil. The accusation judges the player's THEORY against locked facts,
> then frames the consequence. Mean Streets: the game never says "correct."

## How Endings Resolve
- Endings key off **which convergence facts the player locked** (marked clue pairs),
  not a scripted choice. Different proven-fact sets → different interpretations.
- The player **accuses** a cause; the world reacts per the *dominance* they empowered.

## The Interpretations
### 1. "The Scapegoat Stands" (few facts locked)
- Player accepts the official myth: the Mage did it.
- **Consequence:** the cover-up holds; the dead keep rising; the Conqueror thrives.
- *Not evil — just incomplete.* The player was spoon-fed and bit.

### 2. "Mercy's Price" (Priest truth locked)
- Truth: the Priest's miracle binds the dead.
- **Consequence:** exonerates the Necromancer; indicts a saint. Faith fractures.
- The "right" answer costs the world its last comfort.

### 3. "The Living Record" (Scientist truth locked)
- Truth: the Scientist lives, holding the honest logs.
- **Consequence:** the truth could *reopen* the Breach. Reveal it or bury it?
- A knowledge-vs-safety dilemma with no clean side.

### 4. "No Villains" (all three facts locked)
- The full truth: success, not failure; love, curiosity, faith — not malice.
- **Consequence:** there is no one to punish. The player must decide what truth *does*,
  knowing it changes nothing about the suffering.
- The intended thematic peak: understanding ≠ resolution.

## Critique / Scope Discipline
- **POC ships endings 1 and 4** (the floor and the ceiling). 2 and 3 are full-game depth.
- Avoid a "score." Endings are *framings*, not grades — that's what makes the mystery feel authored, not gamed.
